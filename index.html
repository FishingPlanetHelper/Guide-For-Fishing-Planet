How to use the database

Important: “Exact” here means a concrete recommended named setup, not a claim that one rig is universally optimal. Fishing Planet's bite behavior depends on weather, season, time, current, depth and structure, and the official wiki recommends using the in-game bite/forecast charts. Equipment values can also change after updates.

🕐 Time

Use the displayed window as your first test. For trophy/unique hunting, check the current in-game bite chart before spending a whole trip.

⚖️ Balance

Keep rod, reel drag and line within sensible limits. The official guide specifically recommends balanced bait, hook, leader and target-fish sizing.

🎯 Change one thing

If bites are poor, test depth, bait/lure, retrieve or location separately so you know what caused the improvement.

🔄 Updates

This database is intentionally easy to edit. Add your own proven uni/trophy spots and update rows when patches change the meta.
